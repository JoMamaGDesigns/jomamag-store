How to get Meta Credits

Every price in this shop is in Meta Credits. You buy Credits inside the Meta Horizon app on your phone — here is the way that works, step by step:

1. Open the Meta Horizon app.
2. Tap your profile picture — it’s at the bottom center of the screen.
3. Tap the purple diamond at the top right. The number beside it is how many Credits you have now.
4. Tap “Add Meta Credits.”
5. Pick a pack and pay with your usual payment method:

300 Credits — $2.99
500 Credits — $4.99 (30 bonus Credits)
1,000 Credits — $9.99 (200 bonus Credits) — Popular
2,000 Credits — $19.99 (500 bonus Credits)
5,000 Credits — $49.99 (1,750 bonus Credits) — Best value

Then come back here and tap the piece you want.

Careful — Quest cash is different money. Inside the app’s menu there is a Wallet, and it holds “Quest cash.” Quest cash does not buy avatar clothes here. Only buy Meta Credits, from the purple diamond. The gold coin at the top of your profile is quest-rewards money — that isn’t Credits either.

Don’t have the app yet? Get it here — or, if the app is already on your phone and a store page opens instead, tap Open or Play on that page to go into the app. That hop is normal; the store page is only for installing.
