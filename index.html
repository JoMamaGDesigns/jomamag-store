How to buy — the way that works

Every confirmed sale in this shop’s history started the same way: Jo Mama G sent the buyer a direct link, they tapped it, and bought the piece. Follow that door — it’s the proven one.

1

Tap the link

On your phone or in your headset, tap “Click here for more details” on the piece you want here — or the link Jo Mama G sends you in chat. Use a phone with the Meta Horizon app installed, or your headset.

2

Land on the piece

The link opens that exact item’s page in Horizon — no searching, no scrolling through other creators’ pieces.

3

Check the name

Under the item you’ll see the creator name. Yours should read Jo_Mama_G. That’s how you know it’s really hers.

4

Buy & wear

Buy with Meta Credits on the item page, then wear it straight away or find it later in your avatar closet.

Please don’t rely on search. Searching an item name — or “Jo_Mama_G” — inside Edit Avatar does not surface this line; Meta has never published how that search ranks, and in testing it returned other creators’ pieces instead. The direct link is the door. You can also visit Central Shop in Horizon Central (the island to the left of Central Plaza) to browse curated creator clothing in-world — selection there is chosen by Meta.

Special requests

Made just for you

Have a special request — a tee, pants, a hat, a whole outfit? Tell me. If it can be made, I’ll try to create it.

Jo Mama G takes commissions — a suit built from your photo, armour for your avatar, a shirt with your name in rhinestones across a knit hat band. Recent commissions include a one-of-a-kind clown suit and matching boots, ice-warrior armour, and the Golden Nina Nine long sleeve.

How it works: first come, first served.

1. You tell her what you want — for a personalised piece like the name hats, your name spelled exactly as you want it to say.
2. She makes the finished piece.
3. She sends the finished piece to you for your approval first — you see the actual product, exactly as it will be worn, before anything is published.
4. Nothing is published until you say it’s right. Only then is it listed, and she sends you your own direct link to buy it.

No surprises — the piece you approve is the piece you buy.

A note on special requests: I consider every request personally. I can’t promise every one — it depends on what it entails — but I will always try my best, and if I can’t make it happen, I’ll let you know.

Ask for yours

Find Jo_Mama_G in Meta Horizon and message her in-world or through Horizon chat with what you’re dreaming of — a photo or reference helps her see it exactly as you do.

Tell her the colours, the occasion, and the name you want on it if it’s personalised. She’ll confirm it’s in the queue and keep you posted as it’s built, stage by stage.

Email Jo Mama G

Prefer email? Write to jomamagdesigns@gmail.com — one tap opens a fresh email with “Special Request” already in the subject line. Tell her your name spelled exactly as you want it to say, the colours, and the occasion.

Simple friend tees sit around 100 Meta Credits; big, time-consuming outfits are priced for the work involved — she’ll agree the price with you before publishing.
